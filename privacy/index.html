Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy covers games published by yaongisgreat (including Mossy Box: Desktop Paludarium and its demo) and the website yaongisgreat.com.

What our games collect

Our games do not collect, store or send any personal information to us. They do not contain analytics, advertising or tracking code, and they do not connect to any server run by yaongisgreat.

Your progress and settings are saved only on your own computer. For Mossy Box, save files are kept in %APPDATA%\MossyBox on Windows. You can delete them at any time.

Stores and platforms

If you buy or play our games through a store such as Steam or the Microsoft Store, that store may collect information about you (for example your account, purchases and play time) under its own privacy policy. We do not control that information. Please see the privacy policy of the store you use:

  • Steam (Valve) Privacy Policy
  • Microsoft Privacy Statement

This website

yaongisgreat.com does not use cookies, analytics or advertising. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data such as your IP address for security and operation, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the fonts are downloaded. The site remembers your language choice in your browser's local storage only.

Emails you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you, and we do not share it with anyone. You can ask us to delete your emails at any time.

Children

Our games do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: yaongisgreat@yaongisgreat.com
